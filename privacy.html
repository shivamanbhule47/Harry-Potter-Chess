Privacy Policy

App: Sunfire & Moonshadow Chess
Developer: Shivam
Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Sunfire & Moonshadow Chess app and web game handle information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The game has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in your device's app or browser storage. It is not sent to us.

  • Game clock and AI level settings
  • Game speed and next-duel preference
  • Match score
  • A copy of the game files, so it can work offline

You can delete this data by clearing the storage of the app or of the browser that runs it.

Device access

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. It may keep your screen awake while you are watching a match.

Hosting and third parties

The game files and this page are served by GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The app store you download from handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
